⚖️ Technology Decision Record

Justified choices for Redis, PostgreSQL, and Node.js vs rejected alternatives like Kafka and SQS.

Mitigation for Target Slowdown, Cache Stampedes, and Queue Overflows via Circuit Breakers and Redlock.

📊 Observability & Rollbacks

Four golden signals tracking p95 latency, error rates, and automated Canary deployment rollbacks.
